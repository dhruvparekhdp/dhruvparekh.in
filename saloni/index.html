Our story

Crafted with Intention,
Worn with Grace

Saloni Collection began at a kitchen table with a love of textiles and a stubborn belief that clothes should feel as good as they look. Every piece is chosen by hand, made in small runs, and packed by us — never a warehouse.

Explore the Collection
✦

Small-batch by design

We make a few of each piece, not thousands. It keeps the quality high and the waste low — and it means what you wear stays a little bit rare.

◈

Honest materials

Cotton, silk, linen and organza sourced from weavers we know by name. No blends hiding behind vague labels.

❋

Packed with care

Every order is folded in tissue, wrapped in a reusable cloth bag, and sent with a handwritten note. Cash on delivery available across India.

Questions? Say Hello

Sizing help, fabric questions, custom orders — we answer every message ourselves, usually within a day.
