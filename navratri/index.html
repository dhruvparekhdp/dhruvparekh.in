        👗 Friday 16th Oct — Traditional Day (clarified)

        It’s Green day and Traditional Indian Wear day. Wear any traditional outfit — saree, kurta, lehenga, chaniya choli, salwar, sherwani, Nehru jacket, dhoti — all count.

        • +2 per member in traditional wear
        • Traditional in green? You also earn the colour point (+1) — so it stacks to +3 each

        ⚖️ Fair play

        • Points are given only by the cultural team, after the day happens — no advance scoring.
        • The system blocks double-scoring, out-of-range points and snack points for a non-host team.
        • Every award and every removal appears in the public Transparency log with time and reason.
        • Counts are taken at 5 PM snack time. Be there to be counted!
        • Day 1, 7 and 8 are holidays/weekend — wear the colour for fun, no points.
        • Judges’ decision is final — but bribes in the form of jalebi are always welcome 😄
