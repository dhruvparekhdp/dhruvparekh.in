          How scoring works

          👤 Individual score

          ✓ Wore the day’s colour (marked at 5 PM attendance)+1
          ✗ In office but not in the colour−1
          · Absent / on leave0

          Individual = (days wore ✓) − (days not in colour ✗)
          6 scoring days → range −6 to +6. Wore all 6 = 🌟

          👥 Team score

          👗 Dress code — sum of the 7 members’ ✓ (7 × 6 days)up to +42
          🍿 Snacks — host team only, on its day, judged 0–10up to +10
          🌟 Extra bonus — for something extraordinary, reason shown publicly (whole festival)up to +8
          ✗ Not in colour — −1 for every ✗ of a member−1 each
          ⛔ Task not done — e.g. no snacks by 5 PM (−10), other misses; reason shown publicly−1 to −15

          Team = Σ members’ ✓ + Snacks + Extra bonus − Σ members’ ✗ − Penalties
          Best possible: 42 + 10 + 8 = 60. Leaderboard shows +earned and −negative separately.

          Ties share the same rank. Scoring days: Mon 12, Tue 13, Wed 14, Thu 15, Fri 16, Mon 19 Oct. Sun 11, Sat 17, Sun 18 = no points.

          ⚖️ Fair play

          • Points are given only by the cultural team, after the day happens — no advance scoring.
          • The system blocks double-scoring, out-of-range points and snack points for a non-host team.
          • Every award and every removal appears in the public Transparency log with time and reason.
          • Counts are taken at 5 PM snack time. Be there to be counted!
          • Day 1, 7 and 8 are holidays/weekend — wear the colour for fun, no points.
          • Judges’ decision is final — but bribes in the form of jalebi are always welcome 😄
